How the comparison is calculated

Baseline: five years at unchanged source prices and unchanged FX. USD values are rounded for display. Future contracts, discounts and renewals are not predicted.

Five-year known fees = upfront purchase + five annual subscriptions/data fees + paid maintenance renewals. Where the first maintenance year may already be included, a 4–5 paid-year range is shown. Odoo's first-year discount is modeled separately.

Unknown remains unknown: implementation, hardware/cloud compute, storage, operations, usage beyond entitlements, tax and external datasets are excluded unless the row explicitly includes them. A blank total for a quote-only product is never treated as zero.

Growth control: applies a hypothetical yearly increase to recurring fees from year 2. The purchase fee stays fixed. This is sensitivity analysis, not a forecast. A frozen-major-version purchase is marked in its row.

Enterprise scenarios: assume 100 or 1,000 actual paid full users. Employees, devices, providers, processors, viewers and creators are not interchangeable. The table shows an undiscounted license benchmark, with actual eligibility, roles, volume agreements and implementation still to be confirmed.

Exchange rates: . Fixed from ECB reference rates, 23 October 2026. Historical prices are converted at this common comparison date, not their historical transaction date.

Procurement context: mixed-scope one-year packages retain their original contract amounts. No five-year extrapolation is shown for packages with unknown seat counts or bundled one-time training.
